Designed for updates

The notices you need, in one place.

The app retrieves data directly from Khalisani Mahavidyalaya's public notice website, then presents it in a convenient mobile experience.

  • Examination, academic and tender notices
  • Local notice history
  • New notice notifications
  • Direct notice and PDF viewing
  • Pull to refresh
  • Last successful update timestamp
  • No advertisements or account required

How it works

From the public portal to your device.

The app contacts the college's public notice system using your device's internet connection.

Privacy

Built without an account or ads.

The app does not need an account for normal use. Notice state and settings are stored locally on the device, and the app contacts the college's public website directly to retrieve notices.

Important: Intense Softwares does not operate a server for notice delivery. The app uses the user's internet connection to reach the public college notice portal.
