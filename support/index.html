Help with refreshing notices, notifications, PDFs and common Android background restrictions.

Common issues

A few things to try first.

KMV Notice Board reads publicly available notices from the college's notice system. Availability can depend on the source website and your device's connection.

Refreshing notices

Check that the device is connected to the internet, then open the app and pull down to refresh. This performs an immediate check using the public college notice portal.

Notifications

Allow notifications for KMV Notice Board in Android settings. If alerts are delayed, check that notifications have not been turned off for the app.

PDF viewing

Some public notices link to a PDF or external document. Make sure the device has an internet connection, then try opening the notice again.

Android background work

Background checks can be delayed.

Android battery optimization and background restrictions may postpone automatic notice checks. Opening the app and pulling down to refresh performs an immediate check.

For more consistent notification delivery, review the app's notification and battery settings in Android. Settings names vary by device manufacturer and Android version.
