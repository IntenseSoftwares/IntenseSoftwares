KMV Notice Board

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how KMV Notice Board, developed by Intense Softwares, handles information when the app is used.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

In this policy

  1. Information collected
  2. Network access
  3. Notifications
  4. Local storage
  5. PDFs and documents
  6. Third parties
  7. Advertising
  8. Children's privacy
  9. Changes to this policy

1. Information collected

KMV Notice Board does not require account creation for its core functionality. The app does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal information for normal use.

Some application state may be stored locally on the device so that the app can work as intended. This can include seen notice identifiers, successful update timestamps, rating-prompt preferences and similar app settings.

2. Network access

The app connects directly to Khalisani Mahavidyalaya's public notice portal to retrieve publicly available notices. These connections are made from the user's device using the user's network connection.

Intense Softwares does not proxy these notice requests through a developer-operated backend and does not operate a server for notice delivery.

3. Notifications

The app may request Android notification permission. If permission is granted, notifications may be used to alert the user about newly detected public notices.

4. Local storage

Local app data may include:

  • seen notice identifiers;
  • the last successful update time;
  • app-open or rating-prompt state; and
  • similar settings needed for the app experience.

This information is stored on the device rather than in a developer-operated online account or database.

5. PDFs and documents

When a user opens a notice, the app may retrieve public notice PDFs or other documents from the college's notice system. Intense Softwares does not claim ownership of those documents.

6. Third parties

The app may open Google Play when a user chooses to rate, install or update the app. It may also open external websites when a user selects a link. Those destinations handle information according to their own policies.

KMV Notice Board does not intentionally sell personal data.

7. Advertising

KMV Notice Board does not display advertisements.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require accounts or intentionally collect personal information for normal use, it is intended to limit the information needed to provide its core notice-viewing functionality.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior changes. The date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.

For support or privacy-related enquiries, contact contact.intensenetwork@gmail.com .
